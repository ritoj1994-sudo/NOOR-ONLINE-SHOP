Privacy Policy — NOOR ONLINE SHOP

Effective date: 19 November 2026

NOOR ONLINE SHOP is an online shopping application intended for customers in Bangladesh. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Order information: customer name, mobile phone number, delivery address, ordered products, quantities and order total.
  • Payment reference: if you choose bKash or Nagad, the transaction reference/ID you enter. The app does not collect your mobile banking PIN, password or OTP.
  • Account information: the store administrator's email address and authentication information used to secure the admin area.
  • Product images: an administrator may choose an image from the device to upload as a product photo. The app requests photo-library access only for this feature.
  • Cart information: shopping cart contents may be stored locally on the device to preserve the cart between app sessions.

How information is used

Order details are used to receive, process, verify and deliver orders, contact customers about an order, manage stock, provide order tracking and prevent misuse. Administrator information is used to authenticate and manage the store. Product images are used to display items for sale.

Payments

Cash on delivery, bKash and Nagad options may be shown. bKash and Nagad are currently handled manually using a transaction reference supplied by the customer. This app does not currently provide an integrated payment gateway and does not ask for banking PINs or OTPs. Never share your PIN or OTP with the store.

Service providers and storage

The app uses Supabase for database, authentication, image storage and server-side order functions. Information submitted through the app is transmitted to and stored by Supabase to provide these services. Supabase may process data on infrastructure outside Bangladesh, subject to its service terms and security practices.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Order information is accessible to authorized store administrators and may be shared with delivery personnel only when needed to fulfil an order or when required by law. Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed for order management, customer support, legal obligations and security, then deleted or anonymized where practicable.

Security

We use access controls and the security features provided by our backend service. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use the official app and do not share account passwords, PINs or OTPs.

Children

The app is a general shopping service and is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted information may contact us to request review and deletion.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information associated with an order. We may need to retain certain records where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version and effective date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the store administrator at ritoj1994@gmail.com.
